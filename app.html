    Calculation method

    Where the Sun and Moon positions (tithi, nakṣatra, yoga, karaṇa, saṅkrānti, month) come from. Sunrise, moonrise and lagna always use the modern ephemeris.

    Every option except Drik computes the Sun and Moon by its own text's rules and does not use the Ayanāṃśa below. Expect the Sūrya Siddhānta and Vākya options to differ from Drik by up to a few degrees (hours of tithi time); Ketkar's stays within minutes of it.

    Yogas

    The yogas follow Muhūrta Cintāmaṇi. Daily windows show on the Good / Bad Times ring; tithi-vāra-nakṣatra yogas in the Secondary yogas view; details in Today Details.

    Local practices, shown in the Secondary yogas view only if ticked.

    Ayanāṃśa

    Raman and KP are approximate; true Citrapakṣa isn't implemented.
