Settings

Alarms

    Alarms are saved as soon as you set them (no need to press Save) and follow real time, even while the dial shows another day. They ring while the clock is open - after a reload, tap the page once so the browser allows sound.

    Time

    Live (following real time)

    Location

    Ayanāṃśa

    Only Lahiri is implemented in this prototype; see js/engine/ayanamsa.js.

    Sunrise definition

    Switchable ring order

    The order Switch display cycles through. Untick an element to leave it out. The good and bad times (Rāhu Kālam, Yamagaṇḍa, Gulika, Durmuhūrta, Varjyam; Abhijit, Pradoṣa, Godhūli, Amṛta Kālam; and the Maraṇa, Viṣa, Amṛta, Sarvārtha Siddhi and Ravi yogas) share the always-on Good / Bad Times ring: good windows on its outer half, bad ones on its inner half.

      Marker lines

      Candrodaya/Candrāsta (moonrise/moonset) appear only when they fall within the day; Saṅkrānti only on the day the Sun enters a new rāśi.

      Defaults

      Puts every setting above (location, ayanāṃśa, sunrise, ring order, marker lines) back to its default. Nothing changes until you Save.
