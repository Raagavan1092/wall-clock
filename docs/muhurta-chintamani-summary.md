# Muhurta Chintamani — summary of Prakaranas 1–2

Source: *Muhurta Chintamani* of Rama Daivajna, with Govinda's *Piyushadhara* (Sanskrit) and Kedardatt Joshi's *Pitambara* (Hindi) commentaries.
Prakarana 1 (Shubhashubha) = book pp. 1–70; Prakarana 2 (Nakshatra) = book pp. 71–154 (Prakarana 1 v. 1–57, Prakarana 2 v. 1–63). Through book p. 70, add 52 to a book page number to get the PDF page (book p. 1 = PDF p. 53). The PDF page offset shifts after that (PDF p. 122 is a two-page spread and PDF p. 123 repeats it; book pp. 128–129 are missing from the scan), so each batch heading gives its own PDF pages.

Verse numbers are as printed (v. N). Book page numbers are shown as "p. N".

**Legend** (asked for):
- **[W]** = a small time window that recurs every day or every weekday (muhurta, kala, yama, varjya ghatis, bhadra parts, etc.).
- **[Y]** = a yoga made by combining tithi, vara and/or nakshatra. It lasts as long as the combination does (usually a day or part of one).
- **[R]** = a rule or list (lords, uses, what to do or avoid) that is neither of the above.

- **v. 5a — reading disputed.** The mula reads "pūrṇā mṛtārkāt", which most readers take as **Mrityu yoga (bad)**. Both commentators split it as "pūrṇā + amṛtā" and quote Kashyapa ("…pūrṇārkāv amṛtā śubhā"). So they read it as **Amrita yoga (good)**, and Kedardatt calls the "mrityu" reading a mistake. Choose one reading before using this table.
- **v. 5b:** the dagdha nakshatras are bad for marriage, travel and similar work (Narada, Lalla).

**Personal prohibitions (v. 7) [R]**

| Tithi | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Shashthi (6) | Oil (oil massage) |
| Ashtami (8) | Meat |
| Chaturdashi (14) | Shaving and hair-cutting |
| Amavasya and Purnima (and, per the commentary, all parvas) | Sexual union |
| 2, 10, 13 | Abhyanga or udvartana (rubbing with paste) |
| 7, 9, Amavasya | Bath with amalaka (amla) paste (other sources add more days) |

- The parvas are Krishna 8, Krishna 14, Amavasya, Purnima and the Sankranti day.
- Some texts also forbid oil massage on vyatipata, sankranti, ekadashi, parvas, bhadra and vaidhriti.
- For bathing, oil massage, tooth cleaning, union, birth and death, use the tithi current at that moment, not the one at sunrise. Example: if the 6th ends 1 hour after sunrise, an oil bath is allowed after that.

**v. 32 commentary**
- Eclipses: Vasishtha avoids 8 days for a total eclipse, 6 for three-quarters, 4 for half and 3 for a quarter. Garga avoids 7 days before and after. Daily, occasional and kamya japa and homa are not affected by an eclipse.
- Utpatas are of three kinds: divya (comets), antariksha (gandharva-nagara, rainbows) and bhauma (earthquakes). Things that match the season are good; things against the season are portents.
- Planetary war has four kinds: bheda, ullekha, amshu-mardana and apasavya.
- An eclipse that falls on a nakshatra junction: avoid both nakshatras.
- Two eclipses in one month: the first is merged into the second, so avoid the second eclipse's nakshatra for 6 months.

### Batch 3 — book pp. 41–71 (PDF pp. 93–122; PDF p. 122 is a two-page spread of book pp. 70–71, and PDF p. 123 repeats it)

| 37 | **[W]** Four bad daytime muhurtas: Kulika, Kalavela, Yamaghanta and Kantaka. Count the weekdays from today to a set weekday, both ends included, and double the count. Commentary: of the 11 karanas, 7 are moving (Bava, Balava, Kaulava, Taitila, Gara, Vanija, Vishti) and 4 are fixed (Shakuni, Chatushpada, Naga, Kimstughna, from Krishna 14 second half to Shukla 1 first half). Work suited to Bhadra: killing, binding, poison, fire, weapons, cutting, driving away, and work with horses, buffaloes and camels. Bhadra's 30 ghatis by body part (Kashyapa): mouth 5 (loss of the work), throat 1 (death), chest 11 (poverty), navel 4 (falling), waist 6 (madness), tail 3 (sure victory). Scale these to the actual length of the Bhadra. Ratnamala gives the direction of the mouth by prahara; do not travel toward it. |

Moon in Mesha, Vrishabha, Mithuna or Vrishchika: in heaven, so little harm on earth. Moon in Kanya, Tula, Dhanu or Makara: in the nether world, so its result stays there. When Bhadra is in heaven or the nether world, urgent work may be done. |

| 54 | **[W]** When the weekday begins (vara-pravritti). The weekday does not begin at local sunrise but at the moment of sunrise on the prime meridian (Lanka, Ujjain, Kurukshetra, Meru).
